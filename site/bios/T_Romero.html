Tony Romero is a Computational Mathematics, Science and Engineering PhD student at Michigan State University. After receiving his B.S. in Astrophysics from Michigan State University in 2022, he worked as a scientist at Los Alamos National Laboratory, developing data-processing and simulation software for the NNSA's Spaced-based Nuclear Detonation Detection (SNDD) program. His current research focuses on developing magnetohydrodynamic simulations of terrestrial and astrophysical plasma jets, as well as applying machine learning to magnetic turbulence.
